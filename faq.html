Frequently Asked Questions

Short answers to common product, integration and security questions.

What is Orbis1?

Orbis1 provides application-layer tooling that makes USDT on Bitcoin + Lightning production-ready. RGB powers the asset layer; Lightning is used where payments matter.

Do you custody funds?

No. Orbis1 is non-custodial and integrates with your existing wallet/custody setup.

Do you provide a consumer wallet?

No. Orbis1 is tooling for developers, businesses, and merchants.

Do you issue USDT or other assets?

No — Orbis1 does not issue stablecoins. We provide the infrastructure to use them on Bitcoin.

What do developers integrate?

SDKs/APIs for send/receive primitives, invoice/checkout flows, status tracking, and webhooks.

Why Bitcoin / Why RGB?

Bitcoin provides the settlement rail and global reach. RGB enables client-side validation for private, scalable asset flows without adding new chains or tokens.
